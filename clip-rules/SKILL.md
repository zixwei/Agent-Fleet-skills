# Clip rules

Run this list on every clip. A "no" anywhere means fix it or drop the clip. These are practical rules, not legal
advice: using identifiable streamers or game footage in paid promotion needs a real legal check.

## Rights and credit
- [ ] The streamer is named on screen (credit line or header) and in the post, with handle and platform.
- [ ] Credit is courtesy, not a license. Keep excerpts short and transformed: framing, captions, edit, commentary.
- [ ] No implied affiliation: no "official", "partnered", "x <brand>", no streamer face beside a call to action.
- [ ] Provenance is kept (`.clip.json`: source link, start/end, date). Take a clip down quickly if asked.
- [ ] Neither IShowSpeed nor Kai Cenat had a confirmed official clipping program as of Nov 19, 2026. Never write
      "Speed/Kai pay clippers"; agency pools around a streamer are run by independent agencies.

## Rockstar's video policy (as reported; check the current page)
- [ ] Footage from after the game was out (NZ unlock, 11:00 UTC Nov 18, 2026). No leaked or pre-release footage.
- [ ] No endings or major story reveals; no cutscene on its own or cutscene compilations; no in-game TV or
      performance on its own. Show the streamer playing and reacting.
- [ ] No promotion of cheats, mods, VPNs or region changes to play early.
- [ ] No Rockstar logos, key art or the game's wordmark in your own graphics. Naming the game is fine.

## Campaign brief (read each one; they differ)
- [ ] Platform list, minimum and maximum length, footage source (often one stream only), audience geography.
- [ ] Required credit wording and hashtags (for example the streamer's name plus a tag such as `#LACY`).
- [ ] No logos or watermarks of any kind, unless the brief says otherwise.
- [ ] Submission deadline after posting (some agencies ask within 30 minutes).
- [ ] Posts stay up as long as the brief says (often 30 days after the campaign ends).
- [ ] Skip gambling, betting, crypto and cannabis-sponsored campaigns: regulated categories with extra rules.

## Platforms
- [ ] Your own edit, not a re-upload: YouTube does not pay for "non-original" Shorts (unedited clips,
      re-uploads, compilations with nothing added); TikTok keeps unoriginal content out of For You; Instagram
      stops recommending accounts that repost unoriginal Reels 10+ times in 30 days.
- [ ] No bots, bought views, engagement pods, self quote-posts, mass duplicate uploads, scripts that get around
      platform systems, or geo-spoofing.
- [ ] Disclosure: "#ad" at the start plus the platform's paid-partnership setting whenever you are paid or
      given anything for the post (campaign payouts count).

## Brand safety
- [ ] No slurs, hate, sexual content, real-world violence talk, private information or minors at risk.
- [ ] Profanity bleeped when the page or brief wants it (`cut-and-frame`, step 3).

## Honesty in the product claims
- [ ] Say what AI Producer did: here it hosts the project, captions, timeline and export; the cut and the 9:16
      framing were made locally with ffmpeg. AI Producer does not find moments in long VODs and does not turn
      16:9 into 9:16.
- [ ] No earnings claims. Campaigns pay per 1,000 views with minimums, caps and fees; most clips earn $0.
