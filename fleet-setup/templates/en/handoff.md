# Handoff

Append only and preserve earlier records. Name the receiving role and task in each heading. Read the latest section addressed to your role or task; do not simply take the last entry by date. Ask first if its intended recipient is unclear. Treat a compacted summary as a clue; verify the project card, matching handoff and actual state. Label judgments Observed, Inferred, or Guessed.

Record important human decisions, new rules and newly started background tasks immediately in the decision log or handoff; reconcile them at closeout. Only the project manager maintains the project card; other roles propose changes here or in the decision log.

## 〔待定：Date | Receiving role | Task〕

- Progress: 〔待定：Existing artifacts and verification results; distinguish execution ended, delivery complete, and quality accepted〕
- Next steps: 〔待定：The queued items in order, past any milestone; which one to do first if the first item is blocked, for example by a pending human decision〕
- Blockers: 〔待定：Missing materials, authorization, or an error; otherwise None〕
- Who takes over and when: 〔待定：Receiving role and trigger time or condition〕
- On success: 〔待定：How to recognize success and who receives the result〕
- On failure: 〔待定：How to recognize failure, what to preserve, and who decides next〕
- Resume command: 〔待定：Working directory, checks required first, and directly executable command; say if no command is needed〕
- Running tasks: 〔待定：Task identifier, start time, state evidence, how to recognize success and failure, and how to resume; otherwise None〕
- Project card change proposed: 〔待定：Change and evidence for the project manager to check; otherwise None〕
- Human decision needed: 〔待定：Specific action, recommendation, and reasoning〕
