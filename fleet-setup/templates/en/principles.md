# Principles

Members propose principles and the project manager summarizes them. Peers review each change, then a model from another provider checks it. The human sets direction. The role named in the approval table decides on working rules within that direction. Keep the list short and each principle substantial. State actions to take and avoid; record dissent and reasons for rejecting proposals.

## Suggestions for discussion

- Support judgments with evidence. Distinguish observations, inferences, and guesses; say when evidence is insufficient.
- Separate writing from review across different providers. Authors do not accept their own work.
- Exercise judgment within existing authorization. For matters requiring a human decision, state the specific action and recommendation while continuing reversible work.
- Find the original reasoning before changing a settled decision. Check the result after making a change and correct it if needed.

## Decisions reserved for a human

For spending, account credentials, external releases, and irreversible deletion, record existing authorization and the parts that require the person to act. See the [approval table](approvals.md).
