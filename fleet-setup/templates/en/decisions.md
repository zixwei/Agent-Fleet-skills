# Decision log

Append only. When a new decision replaces an earlier one, reference the earlier entry without rewriting history. For a trial, put the same decision ID in the opportunity log. Append the outcome under that ID after the retest.

## 〔待定：Date and entry identifier〕

- Decision maker: 〔待定：Specific project role or owner〕
- Decision: 〔待定：Applicable matter, scope, and conditions for taking effect〕
- Reasoning: 〔待定：Reasons and evidence files〕
- Dissent: 〔待定：Views raised and reasons for their treatment〕
- Replaces: 〔待定：ID of the earlier rule or decision this replaces, or None〕
- Who needs to read it: 〔待定：Affected roles; project manager to check it has been communicated〕

For a trial, fill in these five fields.

- Original review finding: 〔待定：What the reviewer flagged and where to find the original review〕
- Trial rule: 〔待定：The change to try〕
- Trial scope: 〔待定：Which tasks or roles will use it and what the retest will check〕
- Retest owner: 〔待定：Person or role responsible for the retest〕
- Result: 〔待定：Retest evidence, plus where the rule and skill were updated or why the trial was withdrawn〕
