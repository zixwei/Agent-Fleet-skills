# Opportunity log

Add a row when work stalls, someone has to interrupt the human, or the same issue needs correcting again. Link the original review or handoff. If the issue recurs, add the new date and evidence to the same row. Leave the table empty until something actually happens.

| Kind of issue and what happened | Dates and evidence locations | Change to try | Status | Decision ID |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

In the Status column, enter `Idea`, `Decided, not started`, `In trial`, `Verified`, or `Withdrawn`. At the weekly review, select a small trial only if the same issue has occurred at least twice or the role authorized in the approval table agrees to an earlier trial. Enter the decision ID when the trial starts. The retest owner records the outcome under that ID. Mark an effective change Verified. If evidence is still missing, keep the status In trial. If the change did not help, mark the status `Withdrawn` and record one sentence under the same decision ID explaining why the change was withdrawn.
